Electrical System & Capacity Audits

FSR-led assessment of electrical capacity, condition, and code compliance — built for owners planning EV charging, battery storage, or broader electrification upgrades.

Book an Assessment Email About This Project

Know What Your System Can Carry Before You Commit

Adding EV chargers, battery storage, or new equipment starts with an honest read on existing capacity — not a guess. CNCES audits are electrical-specific and code-focused: this is a capacity and compliance assessment, not an energy-efficiency (EnerGuide-style) home audit.

Findings are delivered by the same Field Safety Representative who can carry the resulting permit work — so a capacity study leads directly into action, not another handoff.

  • Load & capacity studies
  • EOP compliance reviews
  • EV & BESS readiness assessments
  • Lighting retrofit analysis

Assessment-Led, Not Sales-Led

A Class A FSR audit reads the system as it exists today — capacity, condition, and code compliance — before any equipment or upgrade decision is made.
