Chargers We Install

Tesla, ChargePoint and other Level 2 chargers

A Level 2 charger runs on 240 volts, the same supply as an oven or dryer. Technical Safety BC puts a full charge at about 6 to 14 hours on Level 2, against 12 to 20 hours on a standard 120-volt outlet.

Tesla Wall Connector installation

The Wall Connector is hardwired to its own circuit, with no plug. We size the circuit for your panel and set the Wall Connector's charging current to match it.

ChargePoint Home Flex installation

The Home Flex can be hardwired, or plugged into a NEMA 14-50 or 6-50 outlet at its 32- or 40-amp settings. Hardwiring unlocks its other settings, up to 50 amps.

240-volt outlets for EV chargers

Bought a plug-in charger, or want to take it with you when you move? We install a NEMA 14-50 or 6-50 outlet on its own circuit, sized for your charger.

Other Level 2 home chargers

We install other Level 2 chargers too. If you want BC Hydro's rebate, check the charger is on BC Hydro's list of eligible models before you buy.

Panel Capacity

Do you need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Often not. Before we quote, we work out how much spare capacity your electrical service has.

Check your meter data

BC Hydro recommends a load calculation from the last 12 months of your hourly meter data. We can't download it for you, so you export it from MyHydro and send it to us.

Add an EV power management device

If your service is close to full, a power management device shares capacity between the charger and the rest of your home, so you can often skip the upgrade.

Upgrade the service if needed

If power management won't cover it, we upgrade your panel and service and coordinate the new connection with BC Hydro.

How It Works

How a home EV charger installation works

  1. Capacity check

    We look at your panel and your meter data, then recommend a circuit size and whether you need power management.

  2. Charger and location

    Pick a spot close to where you park, ideally out of the weather. Garages, carports, driveways and detached garages all work, with outdoor-rated equipment where needed.

  3. Electrical permit

    An electrical permit is legally required for an EV charger in BC. In North Vancouver and West Vancouver it's issued by your municipality, and we apply for it.

  4. Installation

    We install and test the charger, set its charging current to match the circuit, and close out the permit.

  5. Rebate

    BC Hydro's home charger rebate needs proof of purchase and installation within 180 days. We give you an itemized invoice for your application.

Rebates

BC Hydro rebates for home EV chargers

Rebates change, so check BC Hydro's page before you buy. FortisBC electricity customers apply through FortisBC.

Home EV charger rebate

For single-family homes, row homes and duplexes. Apply online within 180 days of installation.

  • Up to 50%of buying and installing a Level 2 charger (maximum $350)
  • Extra $200for single-family homes that install an EV power management device
  • $250 crediton your bill for enrolling an eligible smart charger in Peak Saver, plus $50 each season you stay enrolled
