EV Charging for Stratas and Homes

Whether you need a charger at home or an EV Ready Plan for a whole complex, CNCES handles the electrical side.

Request EV Charging Help Call 604 616 1975
For Stratas

EV charging in a strata

Strata owners can ask for EV charging under a set process. A qualified electrical contractor supports the request and the plan behind it.

After your EPR

Once a strata has its Electrical Planning Report, or its EPR deadline has passed, owners can request EV charging. Learn about EPRs.

An owner's request

Each request needs a qualified contractor's work description, estimate and timeline. The strata council must decide within three months.

EV Ready Plans

A licensed electrical contractor can prepare an EV Ready Plan, which sets out what it takes to bring charging to every parking stall. It works for any building type.

BC Hydro rebates may be available

For complexes of five or more units, with pre-approval and while funding lasts.

  • Up to 75%of an EV Ready Plan (maximum $3,000)
  • Up to 50%of infrastructure (maximum $600 per stall)
  • Up to 50%of chargers
See BC Hydro's program details
For Home Owners

EV chargers for your home

We install home EV chargers with load management. An EV power management device can help you add a charger without upgrading your panel.

  • An electrical permit is required for a home charger
  • Need more capacity? See panel and service upgrades

BC Hydro rebates may be available

For single-family homes, row homes and duplexes. Eligibility is checked per address, and funding is limited.

  • Up to 50%of purchase and installation (maximum $350)
  • Extra $200for single-family homes that install an EV power management device
See BC Hydro's program details
