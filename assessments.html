Strata electrical planning reports

Start here if you manage a strata

Qualifying stratas must obtain an Electrical Planning Report (EPR) by December 31, 2026. CNCES prepares EPRs for townhouse and low-rise stratas.

Request an EPR Quote How EPRs Work
EPR deadline Dec 31, 2026 Stratas with five or more lots in Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District, excluding islands reachable only by air or boat

Load & capacity study

A detailed look at a building's electrical capacity, condition and code compliance, led by a Class A FSR, for owners and businesses planning upgrades. An EPR is only an overview; a study goes deeper.

Lighting retrofit analysis

Review your existing lighting, and what an upgrade would mean for your electrical system, before you commit to a design.

Which one do I need?

  • My strata has five or more lotsStrata electrical planning report
  • I own a building or run a business and I'm planning an upgradeLoad & capacity study
  • I'm thinking about changing my lightingLighting retrofit analysis
  • I want to add EV chargingEV charging
  • I'm planning battery storageEnergy storage & BESS compliance
