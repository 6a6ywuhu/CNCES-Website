For Homes

Upgrades for your home

Adding a heat pump, a suite or an EV charger usually means adding electrical capacity. We serve North and West Vancouver, including the British Properties, Ambleside, Dundarave and Caulfeild.

Panel upgrades

Panel and service upgrades for the loads you're adding. TSBC allows historical load data to inform panel upgrades.

Heat pump circuits

Circuits, panels and service upgrades for heat pumps. Rebates may be available through BC Hydro or CleanBC Better Homes.

Suites and laneways

Adding units? Start your BC Hydro connection request early, since a new service can need a transformer.
