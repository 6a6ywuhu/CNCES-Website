Strata electrical planning reports: due December 31, 2026

Stratas with five or more lots in Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Capital Regional District (excluding islands reachable only by air or boat) need an EPR by this date. We prepare EPRs for townhouse and low-rise stratas.
