What we review

Utility-connected storage has extra requirements: seismic bracing, fire-safety separation, utility interconnection, and CEC/TSBC code rules that vary by battery type.

  • Design review against CEC and TSBC requirements
  • Permitting and utility interconnection coordination
  • Seismic and fire-safety compliance review
  • Field inspection and final acceptance documentation

Not sure your building can take battery storage yet? Start with an assessment.

Proven on a first-of-its-kind installation

CNCES carried the multi-bulletin technical review for a first-of-its-kind nickel-hydrogen battery energy storage system in BC, from design review through code compliance documentation.
