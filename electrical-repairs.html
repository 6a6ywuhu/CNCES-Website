Troubleshooting

Electrical problems we find and fix

We trace the problem back to its cause, explain what we found, and fix it.

Burning smell, sparks or a hot outlet or switch? Stop using it and switch off its breaker if you can do that safely. If there's smoke or fire, get everyone out and call 911.

Breaker keeps tripping or won't reset

A breaker that trips again and again, or won't stay on, is telling you something on that circuit needs attention: too much load, a faulty device or damaged wiring.

Outlets or lights not working

When the outlets in one room, or part of the house, lose power and the breaker looks fine, we find where the circuit has failed.

GFCI outlet won't reset

A GFCI that trips straight away or won't reset can mean a fault further along the circuit, moisture in an outdoor outlet, or a worn-out device.

Flickering or dimming lights

One flickering fixture is a different problem from lights flickering across the house. We check the fixtures, the connections and the circuits feeding them.

Burning smell, sparks or warm outlets

Treat these as urgent. In one Technical Safety BC investigation in Port Coquitlam, a portable air conditioner on a shared circuit contributed to a short that caused fire damage inside an outlet box.

Electrical safety check

Bought an older home, or have a home inspection report with electrical items? We check the panel, wiring and outlets and tell you what needs work.
