What FSR of record covers

Your FSR of record is the licensed Field Safety Representative responsible for your electrical permits and compliance records. We carry them end to end.

  • Permit applications and declarations
  • EOP lifecycle documentation
  • EOP compliance reviews
  • Ongoing compliance record-keeping
