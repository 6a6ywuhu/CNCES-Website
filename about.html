About CNCES

A Credential-Led Electrical Contractor for the North Shore

Canadian North Coast Electrical Services Ltd. is a Red Seal, Class A FSR electrical contractor based in North Vancouver, BC — built around one Field Safety Representative carrying compliance documentation from first design review to final acceptance.

Led by a Class A Field Safety Representative

CNCES is owned and operated by Wade Hickey, a Red Seal Electrician and Class A FSR. Class A is the highest FSR classification available — carrying no voltage or capacity ceiling — and Wade has held FSR-of-record responsibilities across battery storage compliance, strata infrastructure, estate properties, commercial tenant improvements, and mobile vendor electrical throughout his career.

That range is by design. Rather than treating compliance as a formality attached to the end of a job, CNCES structures every engagement around the documentation an inspector, a strata council, or an insurer will eventually ask to see.
